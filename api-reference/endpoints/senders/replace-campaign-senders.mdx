---
openapi: put /campaigns/{campaignId}/senders
description: Replaces the sender pool of a campaign.
---

<Note>
The sender pool requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

<Tip>
To pin the sender of one step, outside the pool, see
[Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) in the
Sequences group.
</Tip>

Replaces the pool with the senders of the body. Each entry follows the rules of
[Add Campaign Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/add-campaign-senders).

<Warning>
A sender or a channel that is not in the body leaves the pool. To add one
sender, use [Add Campaign Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/add-campaign-senders).
An empty list (`{ "senders": [] }`) clears the pool.
</Warning>

## Effects on the leads

- The leads not contacted yet get a sender of the new pool at their next step.
- The contacted leads keep their sender. When their sender or channel leaves
  the pool, their next steps stop with a "Send user is missing" error, until
  the sender comes back in the pool.
- `impact.contactedLeads` gives the number of these leads. It is present when
  the change removes a sender or a channel.
