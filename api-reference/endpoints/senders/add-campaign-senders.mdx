---
openapi: post /campaigns/{campaignId}/senders
description: Adds senders, or channels of senders, to the sender pool of a campaign.
---

<Note>
The sender pool requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

<Tip>
To pin the sender of one step, outside the pool, see
[Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) in the
Sequences group.
</Tip>

Adds senders and channels to the pool. This endpoint never removes a sender or
a channel, so you can send the same request again with no risk.

- Without `channels`, a sender gets the channel types that the campaign steps
  use, healthy accounts first. The campaign editor does the same when you
  enable a sender.
- A sender already in the pool keeps their channels, and gets the listed
  channels.
- A channel type must be used by at least one step of the campaign. Get the
  types in `requiredChannelTypes` of
  [List Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/list-senders).
- Get the user ids, the account ids and the aliases from
  [List Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/list-senders).

On a running campaign, the leads not contacted yet can get a new sender at
their next step.
