---
openapi: delete /campaigns/{campaignId}/senders/{userId}
description: Removes one sender from the sender pool of a campaign.
---

<Note>
The sender pool requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

<Tip>
To pin the sender of one step, outside the pool, see
[Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) in the
Sequences group.
</Tip>

Removes one sender, with all their channels, from the pool. A user who is not
in the pool does not change the pool.

- The leads not contacted yet get another sender of the pool at their next
  step.
- The contacted leads keep the removed sender. Their next steps stop with a
  "Send user is missing" error, until the sender comes back in the pool.
  `impact.contactedLeads` gives the number of these leads.
- A step with this user as forced sender keeps it. To change it, see
  [Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender).
