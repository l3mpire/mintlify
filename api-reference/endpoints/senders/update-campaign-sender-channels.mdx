---
openapi: patch /campaigns/{campaignId}/senders/{userId}
description: Enables or disables channels of one sender, or changes the alias of a mailbox.
---

<Note>
The sender pool requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

<Tip>
To pin the sender of one step, outside the pool, see
[Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) in the
Sequences group.
</Tip>

Changes the channels of one sender in the pool. The changes apply in the order
of the body. The channels that the body does not list do not change.

| Change                           | Body entry                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enable one account               | `{ "type": "whatsapp", "id": "<account id>", "enabled": true }`   |
| Enable every account of a type   | `{ "type": "email", "enabled": true }`                           |
| Disable one account              | `{ "type": "email", "id": "<mailbox id>", "enabled": false }`     |
| Change the alias of a mailbox    | `{ "type": "email", "id": "<mailbox id>", "alias": "<address>" }` |

- A sender outside the pool joins the pool with the enabled channels.
- When the last channel of a sender is disabled, the sender leaves the pool.
- LinkedIn cannot be disabled alone: a sender in the pool always sends on
  LinkedIn. To stop it, use
  [Remove Campaign Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/remove-campaign-sender).
- An enabled channel type must be used by at least one step of the campaign.

When the change disables a channel, `impact.contactedLeads` gives the
contacted leads that keep this channel. Their next steps stop with a "Send user
is missing" error, until the channel comes back in the pool.
