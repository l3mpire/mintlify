---
openapi: get /team/dynamic-sender-exclusions
description: Returns the senders that the dynamic sender strategies of the team cannot select.
---

<Note>
This endpoint requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

A campaign with a dynamic sender strategy (`contactOwner`, `leadImporter` or
`customField:<fieldName>`) takes the sender of each lead from the lead data.
The exclusion list removes senders, or some of their channels, from the senders
that the leads can select.

The team list applies to each dynamic campaign without a list of its own. To
read the list in force on one campaign, use
[Get Campaign Sender Exclusions](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/get-campaign-sender-exclusions).

- An entry without `channels` excludes the sender on every channel, also on the
  accounts that they connect later.
- A user who left the team and a deleted account do not show.
- A team without a list returns `{ "data": { "senders": [] } }`.

<Warning>
These endpoints need an API key linked to a user. A key not linked to a user
gets a `401`.
</Warning>
