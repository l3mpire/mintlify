---
openapi: get /senders
description: Lists the team members who can send, with their channels and the health of each channel.
---

<Tip>
To pin the sender of one step, outside the pool, see
[Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) in the
Sequences group.
</Tip>

Use this endpoint to get the ids that the sender pool endpoints need: the user
ids, the mailbox ids, the WhatsApp account ids, the phone numbers and the
aliases of each mailbox.

- Each channel is evaluated as if a campaign used it. A disconnected account
  shows its diagnostic even when no campaign uses it.
- With `campaignId`:
  - `inPool` tells whether the campaign pool enables each sender and each
    channel.
  - `requiredChannelTypes` lists the channel types that the campaign steps use.
    Only these types can be enabled in the pool.
  - On a dynamic sender strategy, the list keeps only the senders and the
    channels that the sender exclusion list of the campaign allows.

The `diagnostics` codes are the same as in
[Get Campaign Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/get-campaign-senders#diagnostics).
