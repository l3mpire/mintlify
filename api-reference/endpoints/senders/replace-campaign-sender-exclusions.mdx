---
openapi: put /campaigns/{campaignId}/dynamic-sender-exclusions
description: Replaces the senders that the leads of a campaign cannot select.
---

<Note>
This endpoint requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

- `senders: [...]` gives the campaign its own list, which replaces the team
  list. An empty list excludes nobody on this campaign.
- `senders: null` removes the campaign list. The campaign uses the
  [team list](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/get-team-sender-exclusions)
  again.

The entries follow the validation rules of
[Replace Team Sender Exclusions](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/replace-team-sender-exclusions).
A refused request writes nothing.

The campaign must use a dynamic sender strategy (`contactOwner`,
`leadImporter` or `customField:<fieldName>`). Else the request answers `409`.
Change the strategy with
[Update Campaign Settings](/api-reference/endpoints/campaigns/update-campaign-settings).

Only the leads reviewed or launched after the change get a sender from the new
list.
