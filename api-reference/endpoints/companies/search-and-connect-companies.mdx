openapi: post /companies/crm/search-and-connect
description: Searches your connected CRM for companies and links each one to the CRM record found, without creating anything there.
---

Searches your connected CRM for each company and links it to the CRM record found — the **Search & connect** action of the Companies page. Nothing is ever created in the CRM: use `POST /companies/crm/create` for that.

Target the companies with `idsOrDomains` (lemlist ids or domains) **or** with `listId` for a whole company list, static or dynamic: 10,000 companies at most either way, a domain counting every company that carries it.

## Asynchronous: read the outcome later

The call queues the work and answers at once with what was **queued**, not with what the CRM returned. Each company's outcome lands on its `crmSync` block shortly after:

- `GET /companies?idsOrDomains=...` returns each company with its `crmSync` block (`searching`, `crmRecordId`, `notFoundAt`, `errors`), 100 companies per call.
- `GET /companies?listId=...&crmSyncStatus=` lists the companies of a list by state: `searching` while the search runs, then `synced` (linked), or `not_found` (no CRM record matched). Without `listId`, the filter reads every company of the team: do not poll it.

## What the counts mean

- `alreadyLinked`: already linked to a CRM record, synced with it again instead of searched.
- `toSearch`: searched in the CRM, then linked or flagged `not_found`.
- `notSearchable`: nothing the CRM can be searched with (no domain or LinkedIn URL), left untouched.
- `alreadyInProgress`: a CRM search was already queued for them, not queued twice.
- `notFound`: the references sent that match no company of your team.
