## Finding a company from your CRM

Use `crmIds` (up to 100, comma-separated) with the record ids of your connected CRM, for example `GET /companies?crmIds=123456789` with a HubSpot company id; for Salesforce, pass the 18-character Account id. The answer is the same as with `idsOrDomains`: the companies found in `data`, each with the matched id under `crmSync.crmRecordId`; ids that match nothing are skipped.

`idsOrDomains` takes precedence over `crmIds`; a lookup ignores the list filters and the pagination.
