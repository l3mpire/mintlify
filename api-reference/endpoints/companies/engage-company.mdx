---
openapi: post /companies/{companyId}/engage
description: Opens the account-based (ABM) campaign of a company.
---

<Note>
  Beta: this endpoint requires the ABM beta on your team. Without it, the call
  answers `403` with `BETA_NOT_ENABLED`.
</Note>

Does what the Engage button does on a company: reopens its most recent live ABM
campaign, or creates a draft named after the company, such as `Acme ABM Q4`,
with the company's contacts already added as leads. The call takes no body.

## One live ABM campaign per company

Calling again returns the same campaign, with `created: false`, until that
campaign is archived or ended. Concurrent calls on one company are safe: one of
them creates the campaign and the others return it.

A reopened campaign with no lead is filled again with the company's contacts,
since the company may have gained some since it was engaged.

| Code | Meaning |
| ---- | ------- |
| `200` | Done. `created` tells whether this call created the campaign, `shouldReviewLeads` whether its leads were just added. |
| `400` | `companyId` is malformed. |
| `403` | The ABM beta is not enabled. Nothing is written. |
| `404` | The company does not exist in your team. |
| `409` | Another engage of this company is still running. Retry in a moment. |
