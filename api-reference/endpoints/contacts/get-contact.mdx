---
openapi: get /contacts/{idOrEmail}
description: Retrieves a specific contact by their ID or email address.
---
import {SnippetObjectReference} from '/snippets/snippet-object-reference.jsx'

<SnippetObjectReference objectName="Contact" objectPath="contact" />

The path takes a lemlist contact id (`ctc_xxx`) or an email address. To find a contact from its CRM record id, use [`GET /contacts?crmIds=`](/api-reference/endpoints/contacts/get-many-contacts).

<Note>
 A contact's `fields` may contain multi-line values with line breaks (`\n`) when a custom field is of the multi-line text type.
</Note>
