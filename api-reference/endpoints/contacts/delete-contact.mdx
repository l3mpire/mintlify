---
openapi: delete /contacts/{idOrEmail}
description: Deletes a lemlist contact by id or email. Cascades to leads, opportunities, lists, inbox and activities.
---

Removes a lemlist contact resolved by its id (`ctc_xxx`) or email. Only the lemlist record is deleted — **no CRM-side propagation** is performed.

Deletion cascades: the contact's leads, opportunities, contact-list associations, inbox conversations and activities are removed alongside it.

The request fails with `409 CONTACT_DELETE_IN_PROGRESS` when an enrichment is still running on the contact — retry once it finishes. A missing contact returns `404 CONTACT_NOT_FOUND`.

<Warning>
There is no soft-delete or undo. Once deleted, the contact and its associated leads, opportunities, list memberships, inbox conversations and activities are gone.
</Warning>
