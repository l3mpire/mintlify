---
openapi: put /team/rules-of-engagement/{rule}
description: Switches one team rule of engagement on or off, in two calls.
---

<Note>
The user of the API key needs the "Edit rules of engagement" permission. Team
admins have it. Else the request answers `403`.
</Note>

## Two-step confirmation

1. Send the new setting without `confirmed`. Nothing is written.
   - When the body changes the setting, the answer has
     `"status": "awaiting_confirmation"`, the setting after the change in
     `data`, and its `impact`.
   - When the body changes nothing, the answer is the stored setting in
     `data`, without `status`.
2. Send the same body with `"confirmed": true`. The setting is written, and
   the answer is the setting after the write in `data`.

A confirmed call that changes nothing writes nothing, and starts the background
work of the rule again. Send it again after a failed call.

## Impact

`impact` counts what switching the rule on changes now:

- `campaignCount`: the campaigns that get the link in at least one email.
- `stepCount`: the email step versions (A and B) without the link.
- `draftCount`: the personalized emails without the link.

A count with `isCapped: true` is a lower bound. `value: 0` with
`isCapped: true` means that lemlist could not count in time. When the body
switches the rule off, every count is `0`.

## forcedUnsubscribeLink

When the rule is on:

- The body of every email step, A and B versions, and of every personalized
  email of the team's campaigns must hold `{{unsubscribeUrl}}`. A link in the
  signature does not count.
- A step or a personalized email saved without the link is refused.
- An email without the link is not sent. Its lead waits until the email gets
  the link or the rule is switched off.

Switching the rule on adds the link, in the background, to the email steps and
the personalized emails of every campaign that is not deleted, archived or
ended. Paused campaigns and campaigns not launched yet are included.

Switching the rule off removes no link.

## Validation

- The body takes `enabled` and `confirmed` only. Another property, or a wrong
  type, answers `400`.
- A `rule` other than `forcedUnsubscribeLink` answers `404`. To change the
  exclusion lists, use `PUT /team/exclusion-lists`.
- When the feature is not enabled for the team, the request answers `403`
  `RULES_OF_ENGAGEMENT_CORE_BETA_NOT_ENABLED`, with or without `confirmed`.
- A refused request writes nothing.

<Warning>
These endpoints need an API key linked to a user. A key not linked to a user
gets a `401`.
</Warning>
