---
openapi: post /leads/end/{leadIdOrEmail}
description: Ends a lead in one campaign, as "Stop lead" does in the lemlist app.
---

The lead leaves the campaign's sequence and its pending steps are dropped. An
end cannot be undone: resuming the lead does not bring it back.

- `campaignId` is required: one call ends the lead in one campaign only.
- A paused lead is ended too.
- A lead already ended answers `already_ended` and nothing changes.
- A lead not launched yet (imported, or waiting for review) answers `409`:
  delete it from the campaign instead.
