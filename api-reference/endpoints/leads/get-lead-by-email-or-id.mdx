---
openapi: get /leads
description: Retrieves a lead using their email address or lead ID.
---
import {SnippetObjectReference} from '/snippets/snippet-object-reference.jsx'

<SnippetObjectReference objectName="Lead" objectPath="lead" />

<Note>
You should use at least one query parameter: `email` or `id`. If both are provided, the email will take precedence.
</Note>

<Note>
Set `version=v2` to get the v2 lead object, as an array: `createdAt` (when the lead was added to the campaign), `updatedAt`, and the variables nested under `variables`. Without `version`, the legacy flat body is returned and carries no `createdAt`.
</Note>
