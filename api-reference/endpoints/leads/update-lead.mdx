<Note>
  This endpoint updates the lead and, through it, the contact and the company it is linked to: contact fields such as `firstName` or `jobTitle` are written on the contact, company fields such as `companyName` on the company; only the fields you send change. `updateStrategy` decides what the lead, its contact and its company keep. `overwrite` (default): sent values replace stored ones, an empty value clears. `overwriteIgnoreEmpty`: same, empty values ignored. `fillEmptyOnly`: only empty fields are filled, identifiers, company link, owner and status kept. Kept values are listed in `warnings` (`FIELDS_KEPT`, `params.fields`: lead variables and contact fields by name, company fields prefixed with `company`).
</Note>
