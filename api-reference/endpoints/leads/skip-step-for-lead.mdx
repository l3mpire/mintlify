---
openapi: post /campaigns/{campaignId}/leads/{leadIdOrEmail}/steps/{stepId}/skip
description: Skips one step of a running campaign for a single lead.
---

<Warning>
    This endpoint is **deprecated** and keeps working. Use [Skip Step for Leads](/api-reference/endpoints/campaigns/skip-step-for-leads) instead: it takes 1 to 100 leads per call, each as a lead id or an email, and answers per lead.
</Warning>

The lead is not stopped: the step never runs and the lead continues with the
next one after that step's own delay. A step the lead already ran is
unaffected — nothing already sent changes.

A skip cannot be undone, here or in the lemlist app. Condition steps can never
be skipped (`422`).
