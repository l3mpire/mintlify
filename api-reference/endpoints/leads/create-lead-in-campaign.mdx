---
title: "Create Lead in Campaign"
openapi: "post /campaigns/{campaignId}/leads/"
description: "Creates a new lead and adds it to a specific campaign. Never updates an existing lead."
---

import {SnippetObjectReference} from '/snippets/snippet-object-reference.jsx'

<SnippetObjectReference objectName="Lead" objectPath="lead" />

<Note>
  **This endpoint creates, it never updates.** When the contact identified by the body already has a lead in this campaign, the request is refused with `400 LEAD_ALREADY_IN_CAMPAIGN` and nothing is written, neither on the lead nor on its contact or company. To change an existing lead, use `PATCH /campaigns/{campaignId}/leads/{leadId}`. With `deduplicate=true`, a contact that already has a lead in another campaign is refused with `409 LEAD_ALREADY_IN_OTHER_CAMPAIGN`. Only this parameter drives that check: the campaign's own "remove duplicates" setting applies to the in-app and CRM imports, not to this endpoint.
</Note>

<Note>
  The lead is new, but the contact and the company it belongs to may already exist in your CRM. The `updateStrategy` query parameter decides what happens to their stored values: `overwrite` (default) replaces them and an empty value clears the field, `overwriteIgnoreEmpty` replaces them but never clears, `fillEmptyOnly` only fills the fields they have empty and keeps everything else (identifiers, company link, owner, status included). Under `fillEmptyOnly` the created lead takes the values the contact kept, not the ones in the body: a `jobTitle` of `Founder` sent for a contact stored as `CEO` produces a lead with `CEO`. Under `fillEmptyOnly`, the contact and company values it kept are listed in `warnings` (`FIELDS_KEPT`, `params.fields`, company fields with their `company` prefix).
</Note>

<Note>
  Beyond the standard fields above, **any additional key/value pair you send in the body is stored as a custom variable** on the lead and can be used in your campaign with `{{yourVariableName}}`.

  In the example, `companySize` and `customVariable1` are custom variables.

  Naming rules: only letters, digits, `_`, `-`, space and `#` are kept — any other character (e.g. `.` or `$`) is replaced by `_`. Values are stored as text.
</Note>
