<Note>
  **This endpoint creates, it never updates.** A contact that already has a lead in this campaign is refused with `400 LEAD_ALREADY_IN_CAMPAIGN`, nothing written; use `PATCH /campaigns/{campaignId}/leads/{leadId}` instead. With `deduplicate=true`, a contact with a lead in another campaign is refused with `409 LEAD_ALREADY_IN_OTHER_CAMPAIGN`; only this parameter drives that check, not the campaign's "remove duplicates" setting.
</Note>

<Note>
  The lead is imported as the API key's user. When its company is not in lemlist yet, that user needs the "Create companies" permission, otherwise the request fails with `403` (`{"error": "You do not have the permission to create companies"}`) and nothing is written. A company already in lemlist is linked as usual.

  Likewise, when its contact is not in lemlist yet, that user needs the "Create contacts" permission, otherwise the request fails with `403` (`{"error": "You do not have the permission to create contacts"}`) and nothing is written, company included. A contact already in lemlist is updated as usual.
</Note>

<Note>
  The lead is always new, but the contact and the company it matches are updated with the fields you send; `updateStrategy` decides what they keep. `overwrite` (default): sent values replace stored ones, an empty value clears. `overwriteIgnoreEmpty`: same, empty values ignored. `fillEmptyOnly`: only empty fields are filled, identifiers, company link, owner and status kept, and the lead takes the values the contact kept. Kept values are listed in `warnings` (`FIELDS_KEPT`, `params.fields`, company fields prefixed with `company`).
</Note>

<Note>
  Beyond the standard fields above, **any additional key/value pair you send in the body is stored as a custom variable** on the lead and can be used in your campaign with `{{yourVariableName}}`.

  In the example, `companySize` and `customVariable1` are custom variables.

  Naming rules: only letters, digits, `_`, `-`, space and `#` are kept — any other character (e.g. `.` or `$`) is replaced by `_`. Values are stored as text.
</Note>

<Note>
  Company fields — `companyName`, `companyDomain`, `companyIndustry`, any `company`-prefixed key — reach the **company record** the lead is matched to, the same record every other lead linked to it reads, not that lead alone. Creating a lead with its company is the intended flow; to edit a company on purpose, use [Upsert Company](/api-reference/endpoints/companies/upsert-company).
</Note>

## Linking to a CRM record

Send `crmId` with the id of a record in your connected CRM to link the lead's contact to that record. lemlist links it without searching the CRM, then runs a first sync that follows your field mapping (the CRM wins conflicts by default). The response's `hubspotLeadId`, `salesforceContactId`, `salesforceLeadId`, `pipedriveLeadId` or `attioLeadId` shows the record the contact is linked to.

<Warning>
`crmId` is in closed beta. Unless the CRM ID remap beta is enabled for your team, it is stored as a custom variable like any other key. It also requires an **admin** API key.
</Warning>

The id takes the formats of [Upsert Contact](/api-reference/endpoints/contacts/upsert-contact#linking-to-a-crm-record): a HubSpot, Pipedrive or Zoho numeric id (send a Zoho id as a string), an Attio record id, or a Salesforce Contact (`003…`) or Lead (`00Q…`) id.

This call adds a lead: it never moves a link, and never refuses the lead because of its `crmId`.

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| No lemlist contact matches the lead | The contact is created already linked to that record. |
| The contact the lead matches has no CRM link | It is linked to that record. |
| The contact the lead matches is already linked to that record | The link stays as it is. |
| The contact the lead matches is linked to another record | It keeps its link: `warnings` carries `CRM_ID_NOT_APPLIED`, `params.crmId` names the id sent. To move the link, send the contact's `contactId` and the `crmId` to [Upsert Contact](/api-reference/endpoints/contacts/upsert-contact#linking-to-a-crm-record), or change it from the contact page. |
| Another lemlist contact, which the lead's email and LinkedIn URLs do not match, holds the id | The lead is added without the link: `warnings` carries `CRM_ID_ALREADY_LINKED`, `params.conflictingContactId` names that contact. |

Any other reason the id cannot be applied — an API key that is not an admin's, an id that is not one of your CRM's, no CRM connected, a record missing, merged or converted in the CRM, a CRM that did not answer — adds the lead without the link and names the reason in `warnings` (see the `200` response).
