---
openapi: post /campaigns/{campaignId}/steps/{stepId}/skip
description: Skips one step of a running campaign for every lead.
---

Leads that have not reached the step move on to the next one after that step's
own delay, and leads added to the campaign later will not receive it either.
Leads that already ran the step are unaffected — nothing already sent changes.

A skip cannot be undone, here or in the lemlist app. Condition steps can never
be skipped (`422`).

The response returns as soon as the step is marked, before the pending tasks
are closed: that part happens asynchronously and emits one step-skipped
activity — and its webhook — per pending lead.
