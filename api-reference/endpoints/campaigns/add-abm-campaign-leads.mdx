---
openapi: post /campaigns/{campaignId}/abm-leads
description: Adds People Database people to an ABM campaign.
---

<Note>
  Beta: this endpoint requires the ABM beta on your team. Without it, the call
  answers `403` with `BETA_NOT_ENABLED`.
</Note>

Takes People Database ids, from a People Database search or a contact-sourcing
run's `contacts[].leadId`, and adds each person to the campaign as a lead.

## Filed under the campaign's company

Each new contact is filed under the company the ABM campaign engages, whatever
the person's profile lists as their current employer. That keeps every lead of
an ABM campaign on its account.

Someone your workspace already has as a contact of another company is skipped
and left where they are: the response lists them in `skipped`.

| Code | Meaning |
| ---- | ------- |
| `200` | Done. `addedCount` leads added, `alreadyInCampaignCount` already there, `skipped` people not added. |
| `400` | `leadIds` is missing, empty or too long, or `campaignId` is malformed. |
| `403` | The ABM beta is not enabled, or the API key's user cannot create contacts. Nothing is written. |
| `404` | The campaign is not an ABM campaign of your team, or its company no longer exists. |
