## Campaign-originated messages

A message sent by a campaign carries the step it came from: `stepId`, the stable
identifier of that step, alongside the `sequenceId`, the 0-based `sequenceStep`
position, and `totalSequenceStep`. Prefer `stepId` when you store a reference —
`sequenceStep` shifts when a sequence's steps are reordered.

<Note>
  `stepId` is present on every message recorded from now on; older messages gain
  it progressively as historical records are backfilled. `sequenceStep` is
  unchanged and keeps being returned.
</Note>
