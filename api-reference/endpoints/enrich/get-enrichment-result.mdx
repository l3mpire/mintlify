---
openapi: get /enrich/{enrichId}
description: Retrieves the state and results of an enrichment.
---

This endpoint retrieves the state and results of an [enrichment](/api-reference/objects-definitions/enrich) by its ID. The HTTP status follows `enrichmentStatus`:

| Status | `enrichmentStatus` | Body |
|---|---|---|
| `200` | `done` | `data` holds the results; `failedWorkflows` lists the requested workflows that failed. A cancelled enrichment also answers `done`. |
| `202` | `in-progress` | `data` is empty: poll again later. |
| `400` | `failed` | `code` and `reason` say why. |
| `404` | `not-found` | No enrichment with this ID in your team. |

<Tip>
Prefer [webhooks](/api-reference/objects-definitions/enrich#webhooks) to polling: lemlist calls your endpoint as soon as the enrichment is over.
</Tip>
