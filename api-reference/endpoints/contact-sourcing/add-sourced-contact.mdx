---
openapi: post /contact-sourcing/contacts
description: Turns one contact-sourcing recommendation into a lemlist contact.
---

Takes a `leadId` from a run's `contacts` array and creates the matching contact
in your workspace.

## Filing and enriching

- `listId` files the new contact into a contact list. Without it the contact
  lands in **All contacts**.
- `findEmail: true` also runs email enrichment on the new contact. It spends
  enrichment credits, so it is off by default.

## Why `companyId` is required

The new contact is filed under the account you pass, whatever the People
Database resolves from the person's own profile — that is what keeps a
recommendation on the account it was made for.

Someone your workspace already has as a contact is the one case this does not
apply to: they stay on the account they are already filed under. An Add never
moves a contact from one account to another, so rather than report a creation
that did not happen, the call answers `409`.

| Code | Meaning |
| ---- | ------- |
| `201` | Contact created. |
| `404` | The person is already one of your contacts, or no longer in the People Database. Re-run the sourcing to refresh the recommendations. |
| `409` | The person is already one of your contacts, filed under another account. They were left there. |
