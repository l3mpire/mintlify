<Note>
The signal `type` is immutable — it cannot be changed after creation. When you send `filters`, they replace the existing ones and are validated against the agent's signal type.

On an agent monitoring CRM lists, send `crmListIds.in` back as you read it: the monitored lists change through [Monitor CRM Lists with a Signal Agent](/api-reference/endpoints/watch-list/associate-crm-lists) only. `crmListIds.out` takes the company lists of your team to exclude.

Use `activate` to change the run status: `activate: false` pauses the agent immediately, `activate: true` activates a draft or reactivates a paused one (which re-runs billing and external watcher setup). A draft cannot be paused. Omit `activate` to leave the status unchanged.
</Note>
