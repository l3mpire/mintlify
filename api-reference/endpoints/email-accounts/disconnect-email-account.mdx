---
openapi: delete /user/email-accounts/{emailAccountId}
description: Disconnects an email account from your team.
---

Permanently disconnects the specified SMTP/IMAP email account. This will:

- Remove the SMTP and IMAP credentials
- Pause any active lemwarm warming
- Unlink the account from campaigns if owned by the API caller

Only the owner of the email account, or an admin of the team when the owner is a member of it, can disconnect it. Otherwise the request fails with `403` and nothing is disconnected.

<Warning>
This action cannot be undone. The email account will need to be reconnected from scratch.
</Warning>
