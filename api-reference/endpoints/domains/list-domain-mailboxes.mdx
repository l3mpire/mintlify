<SnippetObjectReference objectName="Domain mailbox" objectPath="domain-mailbox" />

Poll this endpoint to follow mailboxes after [Order Mailboxes](/api-reference/endpoints/domains/order-mailboxes). Canceled mailboxes are not listed.

<Warning>
`active` means the mailbox exists at the provider, not that it can send yet. SMTP mailboxes, and Google Workspace mailboxes on a domain bought or transferred through lemlist, are connected automatically. Microsoft 365 mailboxes, and Google Workspace mailboxes on a domain connected from your own registrar, must be connected by their user in the lemlist app (Settings > Sending) before campaigns can use them.
</Warning>
