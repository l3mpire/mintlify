<SnippetObjectReference objectName="Domain" objectPath="domain" />

Poll this endpoint to follow a domain after [Purchase Domain](/api-reference/endpoints/domains/purchase-domain): it is ready once its `status` reads `active`.

<Note>
Canceled domains are not listed. Domains connected from your own registrar in the lemlist app are listed too.
</Note>
