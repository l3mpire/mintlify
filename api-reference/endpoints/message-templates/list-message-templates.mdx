<SnippetObjectReference objectName="Message template" objectPath="message-template" />

The library is what the Templates page (Templates > Emails) shows: whole
reusable messages saved from that page or from an editor, usable as a step on
any channel, an inbox reply or an extension message. Campaign step bodies are
not part of it — read a campaign's steps with
[Get campaign sequences](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/get-campaign-sequences).

<Note>
`search` is a substring match on the **name only**, case-insensitive; accented
`a c e i n o u y` match their plain letters (`relance` finds `Rélance`).
`tags` is any-of and `userIds` is any-of, both comma-separated or repeated.
Bodies are HTML as stored, with `{{variables}}` left raw.
</Note>
