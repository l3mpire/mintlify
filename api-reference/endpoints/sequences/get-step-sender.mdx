---
openapi: get /sequences/{sequenceId}/steps/{stepId}/sender
description: Returns the forced sender of a step.
---

<Note>
A forced sender requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

A step with a forced sender always sends from the same team member, whatever
sender the lead got from the campaign sender pool.

The response has no `sender` when the step uses the sender strategy of the
campaign. The `sender` has the shape of the
[Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) body.
