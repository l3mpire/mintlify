---
openapi: delete /sequences/{sequenceId}/steps/{stepId}/sender
description: Removes the forced sender of a step.
---

<Note>
A forced sender requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

Removes the forced sender of a step. The step uses the sender strategy of the
campaign again.

- The channel type of the step comes back into the sender pool, as in the
  campaign editor. The response returns the pool after the change.
- On a running campaign, the pending tasks of the step move to a sender of the
  pool.
- A LinkedIn or WhatsApp voice note step always has a forced sender: the
  endpoint answers `409`. Use
  [Set Step Sender](/api-reference/endpoints/sequences/set-step-sender) to
  change it.
