---
openapi: put /sequences/{sequenceId}/steps/{stepId}/sender
description: Forces a team member, and one of their accounts, as the sender of a step.
---

<Note>
A forced sender requires at least the **Email Pro** plan.
</Note>

Forces the sender of one step, with the rules of the campaign editor.

## Which steps accept a forced sender

| Step                                       | `channel`                                   |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Email                                      | Required, `type: "email"`                   |
| LinkedIn steps, LinkedIn voice notes       | Required, `type: "linkedin"`                |
| WhatsApp message, WhatsApp voice note      | Required, `type: "whatsapp"`                |
| SMS                                        | Required, `type: "phone"`                   |
| Call, manual, API and phone tasks          | Forbidden: the step pins a user, not an account |

Delay, condition and other utility steps have no sender: the endpoint answers
`409`.

## Rules

- `userId` must be a team member who owns the account.
- Without `channel.id`, the first healthy account of the type is used. An
  unhealthy account is accepted when you name it. Read its diagnostics with
  [Get Campaign Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/get-campaign-senders).
- `channel.alias` picks an alias of an email account. It needs `channel.id`.
- An InMail step needs a sender with LinkedIn Premium.

## Effects

- The sender pool changes as in the campaign editor: a channel type that no
  other step uses leaves the pool. `senderPoolChange` lists the channel types
  that the change enables or disables in the pool.
- On a running campaign, the pending tasks of the step move to the forced
  sender.
