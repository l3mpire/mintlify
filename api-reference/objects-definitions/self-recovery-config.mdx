---
title: Self-recovery config
openapi-schema: SelfRecoveryConfig
---

The account-level self-recovery configuration. When `enabled`, self-recovery watches the `monitoredSignals` on your mailboxes and, for the strategies listed in `authorizedActions`, automatically runs the matching recovery action when a degradation is diagnosed.

Only `fixDns` and `recover` can appear in `authorizedActions`. `provision`, `replace` and `listHygiene` are diagnosis-only and are never executed automatically.

`digestFrequency` sets how often team admins get the recovery summary email: `daily`, or `weekly` on Mondays, at 8am in the team timezone and only when self-recovery opened a case.
