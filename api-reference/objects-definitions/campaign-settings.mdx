---
title: Campaign Settings
openapi-schema: CampaignSettings
---

All the settings of a campaign, grouped by section. Read them with
[Get Campaign Settings](/api-reference/endpoints/campaigns/get-campaign-settings)
and change them with
[Update Campaign Settings](/api-reference/endpoints/campaigns/update-campaign-settings).

The sender pool and the sender exclusions have their own endpoints, in the
[Senders](/api-reference/endpoints/senders/list-senders) group. The schedules
have their own endpoints, in the
[Schedules](/api-reference/endpoints/schedules/get-campaign-schedules) group.
